August 2026: AI Model Release Roundup

August 2026 delivered an unprecedented cluster of frontier AI model releases, with major labs shipping new models within days of each other. Here's the complete rundown.

The Major Releases

Week 1 (Aug 1-7)

Meta Muse Spark 1.2 — August 5
Meta's open-source AI initiative continued with an updated version of Muse Spark, building on their commitment to freely available frontier models.

Qwen3.8-Max — August 3
Alibaba's Qwen team released the 3.8-Max variant, continuing their aggressive release cadence in the large language model space.

Week 2 (Aug 8-14)

OpenAI GPT-5.6-Cyber — August 10
OpenAI shipped the Cyber variant of GPT-5.6, expanding the GPT-5.6 family (which also includes Sol and Terra variants).

Meta Muse Glimmer 30B — August 10
Meta simultaneously released Muse Glimmer, a 30B parameter model capable of near-Opus-class agentic coding on 24GB VRAM.
