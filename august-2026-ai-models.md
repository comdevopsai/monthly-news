# August 2026: AI Model Release Roundup

**August 2026 delivered an unprecedented cluster of frontier AI model releases, with major labs shipping new models within days of each other. Here's the complete rundown.**

## The Major Releases

### Week 1 (Aug 1-7)

**Meta Muse Spark 1.2** — August 5
Meta's open-source AI initiative continued with an updated version of Muse Spark, building on their commitment to freely available frontier models.

**Qwen3.8-Max** — August 3
Alibaba's Qwen team released the 3.8-Max variant, continuing their aggressive release cadence in the large language model space.

### Week 2 (Aug 8-14)

**OpenAI GPT-5.6-Cyber** — August 10
OpenAI shipped the Cyber variant of GPT-5.6, expanding the GPT-5.6 family (which also includes Sol and Terra variants). This followed a major repricing announcement in late July that reduced costs by up to 80%.

**Meta Muse Glimmer 30B** — August 10
Meta simultaneously released Muse Glimmer, a 30B parameter model capable of near-Opus-class agentic coding on 24GB VRAM — a significant milestone for local AI deployment.

**SpaceXAI Grok 4.6** — August 12
SpaceXAI's Grok 4.6 arrived, shaking up the leaderboard and challenging Claude Opus 5's top position according to multiple benchmarking sites.

**Google Gemini 3.7 Flash** — August 13
Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, continuing their strategy of fast, efficient models optimized for high-throughput applications.

**DeepSeek V4 Pro (0813)** — August 13
DeepSeek officially launched V4 Pro with general availability, featuring a 14× price increase over the Flash variant and introducing peak/off-peak billing. The Pro version followed the July 31 Flash release.
